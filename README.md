 ## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Human Resources (HR) Analytics and Workforce Intelligence solution focused on analyzing employee attrition, workforce distribution, and employee engagement patterns.

Using SQL, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau, this project transforms raw HR datasets into structured, actionable insights that support data-driven decision-making in talent management and employee retention strategies.

The analysis helps identify key drivers of attrition, workforce risk patterns, and organizational performance trends to improve HR strategy and reduce employee turnover.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to perform HR data analysis and attrition modeling to answer critical business questions such as:

What are the key factors influencing employee attrition?
Which departments and job roles have the highest turnover rates?
How does salary impact employee retention?
What is the relationship between job satisfaction and attrition?
At what tenure stage are employees most likely to leave the organization?
How does overtime affect employee retention?

This analysis supports workforce planning, talent retention, and HR decision-making optimization.

## 🔍 Key Insights (Data-Driven Findings)
Employees in Sales and Research & Development departments show the highest attrition rates
Low salary bands are strongly correlated with increased employee turnover
Employees working overtime frequently have higher attrition probability
Workforce attrition is highest among employees with 0–2 years of tenure
Job satisfaction is inversely related to attrition, indicating engagement impact on retention
Certain job roles exhibit consistently higher turnover, indicating role-specific risk factors
